The Art of Beginning

“The hardest part of writing is not finishing — it is starting.”

Led by David C. O’Reilly, an accomplished author and feature writer for Mountain Home Magazine, this workshop invites participants to return to the fundamentals of writing.

There are no screens here. No notifications. No pressure to perform. Only paper, pencil, and the quiet discipline of paying attention.

The work begins simply — with a sentence, a memory, or a fragment — and builds toward something shaped, deliberate, and your own.
