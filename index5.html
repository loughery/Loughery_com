The Art of Beginning

A quiet workshop on journaling and short story writing

About the Workshop

Led by David C. O’Reilly, an accomplished author and feature writer for Mountain Home Magazine, this workshop invites participants to return to the fundamentals of writing.

No laptops. No phones. Just paper, pencil, and the steady practice of observation.

The focus is simple: how to begin — and where to go next.
