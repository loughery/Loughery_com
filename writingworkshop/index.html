About the Workshop

This workshop focuses on the earliest and most important stage of writing: beginning. Through lectures, group discussion, and guided writing time, participants explore journaling, letter writing, and short fiction — all with the simplest tools: paper, pencil, memory, and attention.

The emphasis is not on technology or publishing platforms. It is on returning to the quiet essentials of writing — noticing, remembering, drafting, and trusting the first sentence.

Who Should Come

This event is ideal for beginners, journal keepers, aspiring storytellers, and lifelong readers who have always wanted to write. It is also perfect for anyone who wants to preserve family stories — to pass them down to grandchildren and future generations.

No special background is needed. Bring only your interest, your imagination, and a willingness to put pencil to paper.

"Many of us have shared our lives with our Grandparents and then our Grandchildren — a stretch of five generations. What a gift it is to tell our Grandchildren stories of their Great Great Grandparents." — Workshop Theme
"Writing does not begin with perfection. It begins with attention — with noticing what is before you, and daring to put it into words." — Workshop Theme
