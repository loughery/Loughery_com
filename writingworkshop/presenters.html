Workshop Presenters

Meet the Authors

This workshop brings together award-winning feature writers from Mountain Home Magazine — Pennsylvania's premier regional magazine — for a free afternoon of lectures, discussion, and hands-on writing guidance.

Lead Presenter
Feature Writer · Mountain Home Magazine

David C. O'Reilly

David C. O'Reilly is the 2024 Writer of the Year from the International Regional Magazine Association. He retired to Wellsboro after an award-winning career at the Philadelphia Inquirer and has since become a celebrated feature writer for Mountain Home Magazine.

His writing draws on decades of reporting and a deep love of the Pennsylvania landscape. At this workshop, David leads sessions on journaling, personal narrative, and the art of beginning — helping participants find their voice through the simplest tools: paper, pencil, and attention.

About the Publication

Mountain Home Magazine

Mountain Home Magazine has been telling the stories of Pennsylvania's people and landscapes for years — earning dozens of national awards from the International Regional Magazine Association for writing, photography, and design.

Its writers include retired journalists, professors, farmers, and lifelong locals — all bound by a love of place and a commitment to honest, vivid storytelling. The magazine has proven that some of the best writing in America comes from the people who know their place best.

Visit MountainHomeMag.com
