Workshop Presenters

Meet the Authors

This workshop brings together an exceptional gathering of writers from Mountain Home Magazine — Pennsylvania's premier regional magazine and a perennial winner at the International Regional Magazine Association. These are not just award-winning authors; they are storytellers who have spent careers writing about the people, landscapes, seasons, and quiet moments that define life in Pennsylvania's north-central highlands. From longtime newspaper journalists and New York Times bestselling authors to columnists who find deep meaning in a fly rod, a winter trail, or a handwritten letter — each presenter brings a distinct voice and a genuine passion for putting real life into words. Together, they represent the full range of what thoughtful regional writing can be.

Feature Writer · Mountain Home Magazine

David C. O'Reilly

David C. O'Reilly is the 2024 Writer of the Year from the International Regional Magazine Association — one of the most competitive honors in American regional journalism. He retired to Wellsboro after a long, award-winning career at the Philadelphia Inquirer, where he covered stories ranging from local communities to national events with clarity and heart.

Now a feature writer for Mountain Home Magazine, David brings that same precision and warmth to stories rooted in place — the people, seasons, and quiet dramas of north-central Pennsylvania. At this workshop, he leads sessions on journaling, personal narrative, and the art of beginning: how to pick up a pencil, find a first sentence, and trust what you remember.

Contributor · Mountain Home Magazine

Mike Capuzzo

Mike Capuzzo is a New York Times bestselling author whose books The Murder Room and Close to Shore brought literary narrative nonfiction to wide national audiences. A multiple First Place winner from the International Regional Magazine Association, Mike has spent decades proving that the most compelling stories are grounded in truth, observation, and an unshakeable belief in the power of the sentence.

At this workshop, Mike explores narrative structure, the craft of opening a story, and how writers learn to see — really see — what is in front of them.

Editor & Publisher · Mountain Home Magazine

Teresa Capuzzo

Teresa Capuzzo is the editor and publisher of Mountain Home Magazine, which she has shaped into one of Pennsylvania's most celebrated and consistently honored regional publications. Under her editorial vision, Mountain Home has earned dozens of national writing, photography, and design awards from the International Regional Magazine Association year after year.

Teresa brings to the workshop a deep understanding of what makes writing resonate with readers — how a story earns trust, how a paragraph earns its place, and how the simplest subjects, told with care, can become something lasting.

Feature Writer · Mountain Home Magazine

Fred Metarko

Fred Metarko is a First Place winner for Sports & Outdoor Column from the International Regional Magazine Association. He writes about the natural world of Pennsylvania with quiet authority — trout streams, deer season, the first hard frost — in prose that is unhurried and exact.

Fred's sessions focus on writing from the outdoors and from memory: how to describe a place so that readers feel the cold, smell the pines, and understand why it matters. His work is a reminder that the richest writing material is often right outside the door.

About the Publication

Mountain Home Magazine

Mountain Home Magazine has spent years telling the stories of Pennsylvania's people and landscapes — earning dozens of national awards from the International Regional Magazine Association for writing, photography, and design.

Its writers include retired journalists, bestselling authors, professors, farmers, and lifelong locals — all bound by a love of place and a commitment to honest, vivid storytelling. The magazine has proven that some of the best writing in America comes from people who know their place best.
