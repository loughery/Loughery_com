What to Expect

A welcoming, low-pressure afternoon of lectures, discussion, and writing time. There is no grading, no performance requirement, and no obligation to share your work. The atmosphere is that of a friendly creative conversation.

Sessions are led by award-winning authors from Mountain Home Magazine and are designed for anyone who has always wanted to write — wherever they are in their writing journey.

"Writing does not begin with perfection. It begins with attention — with noticing what is before you, and daring to put it into words." — Workshop Theme

Questions?

The workshop is hosted at The Deane Center in downtown Wellsboro, Pennsylvania. If you have questions about the event, location, or accessibility, please reach out.
