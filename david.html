How to Begin Writing—With Nothing More Than Paper, Pencil, and Attention.

A welcoming workshop for people who want to explore the art of journaling and short story writing, led by David C. O'Reilly, accomplished author and feature writer for Mountain Home magazine.

Coming Summer 2026 • The Deane Center • Wellsboro, Pennsylvania

Every writer begins somewhere. This workshop is designed for people who want to start writing but are unsure where to begin. With paper and pencil instead of screens, participants will slow down, observe more carefully, and discover simple ways into journaling and short fiction.

Whether you have always wanted to write, have started and stopped many times, or simply want a thoughtful creative afternoon, this session offers an inviting place to begin.

Where to Begin

Learn practical ways to start writing when the blank page feels overwhelming, and how to build momentum from simple observations and memories.

Journaling as Practice

Explore journaling as a daily or occasional habit that sharpens attention, deepens reflection, and becomes a foundation for richer writing.

Short Stories from Real Life

Discover how small moments, places, people, and fragments of experience can become the seed of a short story worth telling.

About the Workshop

This workshop focuses on the earliest and most important stage of writing: beginning. Participants will work with the simplest tools—paper, pencil, memory, and attention—to find a starting place.

The emphasis is not on technology, productivity, or publishing platforms. Instead, the session returns to the quiet essentials of writing: noticing, remembering, drafting, and learning how to trust the first sentence.

Who Should Attend

This event is ideal for beginners, journal keepers, aspiring storytellers, lifelong readers who have always wanted to write, and anyone curious about turning everyday experience into meaningful prose.

No special background is needed. Bring only your interest, your imagination, and a willingness to put pencil to paper.
