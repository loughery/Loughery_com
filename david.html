About A Writers Workshop

A Writers Workshop is built for people who have long felt the urge to write but may not think of themselves as “writers.” Some want to preserve family stories. Some want to keep a journal with more purpose. Some simply want to understand how ordinary life can become meaningful writing.

David C. O'Reilly's approach is encouraging rather than intimidating. The workshop helps participants discover that they do not need a degree, a publishing contract, or years of experience to begin. They only need attention, honesty, and a place to start.

Why write at all?

  • To preserve family memories before they fade
  • To make sense of personal experience through journals
  • To leave something meaningful for children and grandchildren
  • To notice daily life more deeply and thoughtfully
  • To discover a voice you may not realize you already have
